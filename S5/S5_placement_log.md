## Operationalisation and proposed amendments (dated 2026-10-25; placement_rules.md NOT edited)

Rules read literally. Where they are ambiguous, the most literal reading is applied and an amendment is proposed to ⟦the main model session⟧ (AM-n). None is applied silently.

**O1 Grade precedence (evaluated in this order for every placed cell).**
1. Unit not placed in the phase → grade "—".
2. No age-matched and no adjacent [taught+tested] support → **Conj** (brief: "whatever the consensus says"; this overrides the rules' "M: … or supporting consensus").
3. P1 = "not met" and the cell is placed only because P2 allows an optional Phase-3 extension (P1-vs-P2 conflict) → **undecided**.
4. P1 = "not established" (no P1 source documents the phase of a prerequisite) → **undecided**.
5. Contrary evidence present (a [taught+tested] record at matched or adjacent band reporting no gain/decline, or a comparison where the group without the unit/representation did better) → **L**.
6. P1 met or n.a. + ≥1 age-matched record + no P4 oppose → **H**.
7. P1 met or n.a. + ≥1 age-matched record + P4 oppose → **M** (age-matched counts a fortiori as "adjacent-age evidence").
8. P1 met or n.a. + adjacent-only evidence → **M**.
- AM-1 (proposed): state in the rules that "n.a." (no prerequisite) satisfies "P1 met" in the grade definitions; that undecided is a grade; and the precedence above. Literal reading applied meanwhile = n.a. treated as vacuous compliance (a prohibition with nothing to prohibit is met). Sensitivity: n.a. ≠ met.
- P4 support never changes a grade under item 2 (evidence is required anyway); it is recorded only.
